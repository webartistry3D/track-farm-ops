# TRACKFARMOPS — SHOT BIBLE
"The Land Remembers" — 90-Second Commercial

Complete shot-by-shot breakdown. Each shot references the Character, Visual, and UI Bibles. Shot numbers follow `[ACT]-[SEQUENCE]` format for easy cross-referencing in `08-Asset-Tracker.md` and `07-Production-Workflow.md`.

**Legend:** WS = Wide Shot · MS = Medium Shot · CU = Close-Up · ECU = Extreme Close-Up · POV = Point of View · O/S = Over-the-Shoulder

---

## ACT 1 — THE WEIGHT (0:00–0:25)

### Sequence A1-01 | [00:00–00:04]
- **Shot:** WS, drone, slow push-in. Dawn mist over farmland, silhouette of a farmer walking a dirt path far below.
- **Lens:** Wide (24mm equiv.), slight compression on push-in.
- **Movement:** Slow forward drone push, low altitude, following the path line.
- **Grade ref:** Visual Bible §2 Act 1 — cool, desaturated, overcast.
- **VO sync:** "Before the tractors. Before the satellites..."

### Sequence A1-02 | [00:04–00:08]
- **Shot:** WS, drone pulls back and up, revealing scale of land.
- **Movement:** Continuous from A1-01 if possible (one unbroken drone move) — pull back reveals the farmer is small against vast, foggy acreage.
- **VO sync:** "...there was guesswork."
- **SFX cue:** Wind swells; single low string note lands as pull-back completes.

### Sequence A1-03 | [00:08–00:10]
- **Shot:** MS. Farmer approaches a wooden fence post, satchel visibly heavy on his shoulder.
- **Lens:** Medium (50mm equiv.), shallow-ish depth to isolate him from misty background.
- **Movement:** Static or very slow handheld drift.

### Sequence A1-04 | [00:10–00:13]
- **Shot:** CU/insert. He sets the satchel down; it falls open. Six notebooks spill out.
- **Lens:** Macro-adjacent (85–100mm equiv.), shallow DOF.
- **Movement:** Static, let the notebooks do the movement (tumbling out, settling).
- **Continuity note:** Reference `08-Asset-Tracker.md` for exact notebook wear states — this is their first and most important establishing shot.

### Sequence A1-05 | [00:13–00:16]
- **Shot:** ECU inserts, series of 2–3 quick cuts. Frayed edges, curling pages, one rain-stained cover, one torn page caught in wind and blowing away.
- **Lens:** Macro.
- **Movement:** Handheld micro-movement on the static inserts; the torn page shot tracks with the wind (whip-pan or fast push as it flies off).
- **VO sync:** "Guessing the rain. Guessing the yield. Guessing what tomorrow would take from us."
- **SFX cue:** Music tightens; paper rustle; page flutter-and-gone sting.

### Sequence A1-06 | [00:16–00:19]
- **Shot:** WS/MS. Cooperative meeting under a tree — 4–6 farmers around mismatched ledgers, frustrated, talking over one another.
- **Lens:** Medium wide, documentary-style framing (slightly imperfect, observational).
- **Movement:** Handheld, naturalistic.

### Sequence A1-07 | [00:19–00:21]
- **Shot:** MS/CU. An investor closes a folder mid-pitch, unconvinced — small, cold, deliberate gesture.
- **Lens:** Medium (50–85mm equiv.), clean boardroom-adjacent light (still within Act 1's cool grade).
- **Movement:** Static, let the folder-close carry the beat.

### Sequence A1-08 | [00:21–00:23]
- **Shot:** Overhead/CU. Government map covered in inconsistent, hand-written pins; 1–2 analysts studying it with mild friction.
- **Lens:** Top-down or steep angle, macro-adjacent on the pins.
- **Movement:** Slow push-in on the map's inconsistency (mismatched pin colors, crossed-out notes).
- **VO sync:** "Because farming has always carried the weight of the unknown. And the harvest that could feed a continent has waited long enough."

### Sequence A1-09 | [00:23–00:25]
- **Shot:** CU/MS. Farmer looks down at his six notebooks scattered at his feet. Beat of stillness.
- **Lens:** Medium, on his face and downward gaze.
- **Movement:** Static. Hold. This is the Act 1 button — do not rush the cut.
- **SFX cue:** Music drops to near silence. One held breath.

## ACT 3 — THE INSPIRATION (1:10–1:30)

### Sequence A3-01 | [01:10–01:14]
- **Shot:** Slow motion WS/MS. Farmer stands in golden light, satchel empty and folded over his shoulder — weightless.
- **Lens:** Medium wide, warm flare-friendly glass.
- **Movement:** Slow motion (see Visual Bible §4 — first and only sanctioned slow-motion moment for the human figure until this point).
- **VO sync:** "This is not software. This is clarity."

### Sequence A3-02 | [01:14–01:18]
- **Shot:** WS. Behind him, the land moves like something alive — harvesters, drones, cattle, warehouses humming in quiet coordination.
- **Lens:** Wide, deep focus.
- **Movement:** Slow lateral drone pass behind/around him.
- **VO sync:** "This is Africa's land, finally speaking with clarity."

### Sequence A3-03 | [01:18–01:21]
- **Shot:** CU. He looks up. Sun flare. Cut to black.
- **Lens:** Medium-close, direct sun in frame for flare.
- **Movement:** Static or very slow push, then hard cut to black.
- **VO sync:** "The land remembers who feeds it. Now, it finally has a voice."

### Sequence A3-04 | [01:21–01:28] — THE FINAL ASCENT (hero shot of the film)
- **Shot:** Continuous ascending drone shot. Starts near the farmer's ridge-line eye level, climbs progressively higher.
- **Reveal order (must be sequential, not simultaneous):**
  1. The farmer's own farm, full extent visible.
  2. Neighboring farms coming into frame.
  3. Dozens, then hundreds of farms — varied landscape types: crop farms, livestock, warehouses, road networks.
  4. Subtle glowing emerald connector lines linking locations (UI Bible §5.3 — dotted connection line style).
- **Lens:** Wide, epic glass, anamorphic flare acceptable on sun position (Visual Bible §4).
- **Movement:** One continuous ascent (or seamlessly stitched drone-to-satellite-plate composite) — this is the single most technically demanding shot in the production; see `07-Production-Workflow.md` for VFX pipeline notes.
- **This shot directly visualizes the "hero shot" concept from `01-Script.md`** — the farmer on the ridge with glassmorphic overlays coming alive across the landscape, extended here into full aerial scale.

### Sequence A3-05 | [01:28–01:30] — LOGO CARD
- **Shot:** Pure black frame. TrackFarmOps logo fades in with a warm gold-to-emerald gradient (see Production Note below for full spec). Tagline builds letter by letter.
- **On-screen text:** "TrackFarmOps — See Everything. Miss nothing."
- **VO sync (final line):** "TrackFarmOps. Every acre. Every asset. Every decision."
- **On-screen CTA:** "Run your farm with confidence — TrackFarmOps.com"
- **SFX cue:** Music resolves on one sustained, hopeful chord. Silence.

## PRODUCTION NOTE ON LOGO COLOR — RESOLVED

**Decision: Gold-green gradient (script-literal path), as specified in `01-Script.md`.**

The final logo card (01:28–01:30) renders the TrackFarmOps mark in a warm gold-to-emerald gradient rather than the flat emerald used in the live product UI. This is treated as an intentional "cinematic" brand moment — a heightened, hero-grade version of the mark reserved for prestige brand film use, distinct from the flat-emerald product UI seen in-app.

**Rationale:** The gold accent pays off the golden-hour color grade of Act 3 (see `03-Visual-Bible.md` §2) and gives the final reveal a warmer, more premium, more emotionally resonant close than a flat emerald card would — consistent with the film's arc from cool/grey (Act 1) to fully warm/golden (Act 3).

**Implementation notes:**
- Gradient direction: gold (`#f59e0b`–`#fbbf24` range) transitioning into emerald (`#10b981`) — gold dominant at the top/left of the mark, resolving into emerald at the base, echoing the sunrise-to-clarity arc of the whole film.
- This gradient treatment is reserved **exclusively** for the final logo card (A3-05) and any end-frame brand lockups derived from this campaign (cutdowns, social versions). It should **not** be applied to any screen-within-screen product UI shown earlier in the film (dashboard, tablet, control room, etc.) — those remain pure emerald per `04-UI-Bible.md`, preserving accuracy to the live product.
- Flag to brand/marketing team: this campaign-specific gold-green lockup should be added as an approved secondary brand asset (alongside the primary flat-emerald mark) for consistent reuse across any future cutdowns, trailers, or promotional material tied to this commercial.
