## 3. POSTER / KEY ART MASTER PROMPT

This is the primary campaign key art — the single image used for outdoor, print, social, and press. It visualizes the "Hero Shot" concept from `01-Script.md` ("ONE HERO SHOT" section) at full payoff: the farmer on the ridge, land alive with restrained glassmorphic overlays, golden-hour grade, gold-green brand resolution. Treat this as the definitive poster reference — regenerate variations from this master rather than starting fresh each time.

### 3.1 Master poster prompt
```
Award-winning cinematic key art poster, vertical 2:3 aspect ratio. An African farmer in a worn canvas jacket stands on a grassy ridge at golden hour, seen from a low three-quarter angle, satchel folded empty over one shoulder, shoulders back, weightless posture. Behind and below him, a vast, thriving farm landscape stretches to the horizon — crop fields, cattle pastures, a warehouse, a tractor — all touched by subtle, elegant glassmorphic light overlays woven directly into the land: a soft glowing emerald ledger card hovering near a distant packhouse, a small glowing inventory tag pulsing above the warehouse roofline, a faint glowing health icon near the grazing cattle, thin translucent data-line threads connecting each point of light back toward the farmer, like a nervous system made visible. No clutter, no giant holograms — the technology is small, quiet, and integrated, never overwhelming the landscape. Warm golden-hour sunlight, soft lens flare over his shoulder, atmospheric haze in the far distance, cinematic 35mm film grain, shallow-to-deep hybrid depth of field keeping both the farmer and the horizon legible. Color grade: full warm saturation, hopeful and premium, in the visual language of Apple and Tesla brand film key art. Bottom third of frame left in clean negative space (dark grounded gradient) for logo and type overlay. Photorealistic, ultra-detailed, 8k quality, no text, no watermark.
```

### 3.2 Brand lockup for the poster (apply after image generation, or as a compositing layer)
```
In the clean negative-space area at the bottom of the poster, place the TrackFarmOps hexagonal sprout-leaf logo mark rendered in a warm gold-to-emerald gradient (gold dominant at top-left, resolving into emerald at the base — matching the resolved final logo card treatment in `05-Shot-Bible.md`), centered, with the wordmark "TrackFarmOps" in bold JetBrains Mono below it in warm off-white. Beneath that, in smaller Inter medium weight, the tagline "See Everything. Miss Nothing." Generous negative space, minimal, premium, Apple-keynote-style composition — the logo should feel earned, not slapped on.
```

### 3.3 Alternate crop — landscape / horizontal (for web banners, YouTube thumbnails)
```
Award-winning cinematic key art, horizontal 16:9 aspect ratio. Same scene as the vertical master: an African farmer in a worn canvas jacket stands on a grassy ridge at golden hour, satchel folded empty over one shoulder, facing a vast thriving farmland landscape below with subtle glowing emerald glassmorphic overlays woven into the land — a ledger card near the packhouse, an inventory tag over the warehouse, a health icon near the cattle, thin glowing connector lines linking them. Warm golden-hour light, soft flare, cinematic 35mm film grain, photorealistic, 8k quality. Wide open composition with clear negative space in the sky area for a horizontal logo lockup and tagline. No text, no watermark.
```

### 3.4 Usage notes
- Generate 3.1 (or 3.3 for horizontal formats) as a clean, text-free photographic plate first; composite the logo/type lockup (3.2) separately in a design tool for full typographic control rather than relying on the image model to render legible text.
- Cross-check the farmer's wardrobe, posture, and satchel state against `02-Character-Bible.md` §1 and `09-Continuity-Bible.md` §1 (Act 3 state: empty, folded, weightless) before approving any generation as final key art — this must match the live-action Act 3 farmer exactly for campaign consistency.
- Glassmorphic overlay treatment must follow `04-UI-Bible.md` §5.1–§5.3 (glass card style, dotted connector lines) and the "no clutter, no giant holograms" restraint principle from `01-Script.md`'s Hero Shot notes.
- Color grade must match `03-Visual-Bible.md` §2 Act 3 golden-hour spec exactly, so the poster feels like a single frame pulled from the finished film rather than a separately-art-directed asset.
