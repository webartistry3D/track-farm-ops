# TRACKFARMOPS — SOUND BIBLE
"The Land Remembers" — 90-Second Commercial

Sound carries half the emotional weight of this film — there is no dialogue, only V.O., score, and diegetic SFX. This document specifies music architecture, SFX design, voiceover direction, and the full mix philosophy.

---

## 1. MUSIC ARCHITECTURE

The score follows the three-act structure exactly, functioning almost as a fourth character. One continuous composition, not three separate cues — themes introduced in Act 1 should return, transformed, in Act 3 (see §1.4).

### 1.1 Act 1 — THE WEIGHT (0:00–0:25)
- **Instrumentation:** Solo low string (cello or double bass), sparse, minimal. No percussion. No harmonic resolution — chords should feel suspended/unresolved.
- **Tempo:** Rubato / free-time, tracking the drone's slow movement rather than a fixed BPM.
- **Dynamic arc:** Starts near-silent (wind + single low string note at 00:07–00:08), tightens gradually through the cooperative/investor/government cutaways, and **drops to near silence** by 00:23–00:25 as the farmer looks down at his notebooks. This silence is the pivot point of the entire film — protect it in the mix, do not let ambient bed fill the space.
- **Reference:** Max Richter's sparse string writing; Jóhann Jóhannsson's restrained low-register cues.

### 1.2 Act 2 — THE TRANSFORMATION (0:25–1:10)
- **Instrumentation:** Clean, modern orchestral strings layered with a subtle electronic pulse — the pulse should feel like a heartbeat/circuit rhythm, tying sonically to the emerald "data pulse" visual motif (UI Bible §5.2).
- **Tempo:** Establishes a clear, steady pulse at 00:25 (tablet power-on) — roughly 80–92 BPM, confident walking pace matching the farmer's now-purposeful stride.
- **Structural beats:**
  - 00:25–00:30: Score begins — sparse, just the electronic pulse and a single sustained string note (mirrors the tablet's light bloom).
  - 00:30–00:54 (Income/Expenses → Inventory → Assets): Strings build in layers, one new instrumental voice added roughly every beat (add a viola line at Inventory, cello counter-melody at Assets) — the orchestration itself "fills out" the way the farmer's world is filling out.
  - 00:54–01:02 (Livestock): Percussion enters for the first time — light, organic percussion (hand drums, shakers) rather than electronic/cinematic hits, keeping the palette grounded in the agricultural setting.
  - 01:02–01:10 (Control room / Ecosystem): Full orchestral swell — strings, brass (used sparingly, this is the one moment brass is permitted), percussion, electronic pulse all present simultaneously. This is the **peak of the score**, timed to land precisely as the VO says "Everyone, on one truth."
- **Reference:** Hans Zimmer's cumulative layering technique (*Interstellar* opening); Ludovico Einaudi's electronic-acoustic hybrid textures.

### 1.3 Act 3 — THE INSPIRATION (1:10–1:30)
- **Instrumentation:** Full orchestra, but the electronic pulse recedes — by this point the "technology" sound has fully integrated into an organic, purely emotional orchestral wash. This is deliberate: technology has become invisible/ambient, exactly as the narrative argues.
- **Tempo:** Broadens — the steady 80–92 BPM pulse relaxes into a more rubato, expansive feel during the slow-motion sequence (01:10–01:18), then rebuilds tempo and density through the final ascent (01:18–01:28).
- **Final resolution:** Score resolves on **one sustained, hopeful chord** as the logo card appears (01:28–01:30), then complete silence before the CTA fades. Do not add a music sting or "brand jingle" — the silence after the chord is more powerful and premium-feeling than any additional cue.
- **Reference:** Explosions in the Sky-style post-rock swells; Jon Batiste/contemporary orchestral-hopeful register (think closing cues of *Apple's "The Underdogs"* or Tesla brand films).

### 1.4 Thematic callback
- The single low string note from 00:07–00:08 (Act 1 opening) should reappear, harmonized and resolved, as part of the final sustained chord at 01:28–01:30. A listener should subconsciously recognize it's "the same note, now at peace." This is the single most important musical detail in the score — flag to composer explicitly.

## 2. VOICEOVER DIRECTION

### 2.1 Two distinct VO registers
| Register | Timecode range | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **"Weathered" voice** | 00:00–00:23 | Low, slow, world-worn. Should sound like a man remembering hardship, not narrating it from a script. Slight rasp acceptable. Long pauses between phrases — let silence do work. |
| **"Resolute" voice** | 00:25–01:30 | Same performer, but the register shifts — clearer, more present, gaining confidence and warmth as the film progresses. By Act 3 the voice should sound almost inspired, but never salesy or hyped. |

**Critical direction:** This is one performer, one continuous emotional journey — NOT two different voice actors. The shift at "Until now." (00:25) should feel like the same man taking a breath and standing up straighter, vocally mirroring the farmer's physical arc (Character Bible §1).

### 2.2 Casting
- Male voice, 45–65 age register (matching or slightly older than "The Farmer" character, but need not be the same person — should sound like it *could* be him, or an elder from the same community).
- Ideally a West African / Nigerian-accented English speaker for regional authenticity — avoid a "generic mid-Atlantic ad voice." Accent should read as authoritative and warm, not exaggerated.
- Avoid theatrical/trailer-voice delivery entirely — no "movie announcer" cadence. Reference: the understated, conversational-but-weighty delivery style of recent Apple and Nike VO work.

### 2.3 Pacing map (see full lines in `01-Script.md`)
- 00:00–00:08: Slow, spacious, one clause at a time.
- 00:08–00:16: Slightly quickens as the notebook imagery accelerates, but still deliberate.
- 00:16–00:25: Builds gravity toward "the harvest that could feed a continent has waited long enough" — this should be the emotional low/heavy point of the VO, delivered with quiet conviction, not sadness.
- 00:25: "Until now." — the pivot. Should be short, clean, almost a released breath. Leave air before and after this line in the mix.
- 00:30–01:02: Steady, clear, confident — informational but warm, like a trusted friend explaining something that works.
- 01:02–01:10: Builds in energy and pitch slightly toward "Everyone, on one truth" — this is the VO's own "swell," matching the music.
- 01:10–01:28: Slows back down, becomes intimate and quiet again by the final lines ("The land remembers who feeds it. Now, it finally has a voice.") — full circle back to the Act 1 register, but resolved rather than weary.
- 01:28–01:30: Final tagline line delivered clean, warm, unhurried — the closing handshake, not a hard sell.

See `Prompts/Voiceover-Prompts.md` for AI scratch-VO generation prompts to use during pre-visualization/edit before final talent recording.

## 3. SOUND DESIGN (SFX)

| Timecode | SFX | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:08 | Wind | Low, constant, slightly cold-sounding (subtle high-pass to feel misty/dawn) |
| 00:07–00:08 | Single low string note | Scored element, but should feel diegetic-adjacent — like the land itself sighing |
| 00:08–00:16 | Paper rustling, page flutter-and-gone | Must be recorded/foley'd with real aged paper — no generic library "paper" sound, needs the specific dry, brittle texture of a worn notebook |
| 00:16–00:25 | Ambient meeting chatter (non-verbal, textural), folder-close, distant wind on the map scene | Keep dialogue-adjacent chatter unintelligible/textural — this is sound-as-atmosphere, not sound-as-information |
| 00:23–00:25 | Near silence, one held breath | This should be an actual recorded breath, not implied — subtle but present in the mix |
| 00:25 | Tablet power-on chime | **Custom-designed brand sound** — should be a soft, warm, single-note tone (pairs with the emerald light bloom). This tone is a candidate for TrackFarmOps' permanent sonic brand identity (a "sonic logo") — recommend registering/reusing this exact tone across all future product notification sounds and app startup. |
| 00:30–01:02 | Tag/scan confirmation chimes (Inventory scan, Asset tag illuminate, Livestock tag scan) | All three should share a consistent, related tonal family with the 00:25 power-on tone — think of these as "children" of the main brand tone, reinforcing sonic consistency across every module |
| 00:38–00:46 | Warehouse ambience | Grain sacks shifting, distant machinery hum, tablet scan beep |
| 00:46–00:54 | Mechanic/tractor ambience | Metal-on-metal tool sounds (soft, not harsh), tractor engine tick (cooling metal) |
| 00:54–01:02 | Livestock ambience | Soft cattle lowing, distant, non-alarming; tablet scan chime |
| 01:02–01:10 | Control room / cross-cut | Subtle glass-room reverb tail on the control room dialogue-free ambience; each of the 8 ecosystem cuts gets a very brief (under 0.5s) location-specific ambience snap (boardroom AC hum, field wind, office quiet) to sell geographic diversity within the fast cutting |
| 01:10–01:18 | Wind, distant machinery hum (harvesters/drones), soft cattle sounds | All ambient sounds here should feel harmonious/orchestrated rather than chaotic — "quiet coordination" per script |
| 01:18–01:28 | Rising wind as altitude increases, subtle whoosh transitions between reveal stages | Sound should physically communicate altitude gain — lower frequency content and more air/wind presence as we climb |
| 01:28–01:30 | Silence but for the final chord | No additional SFX — protect the silence |

## 4. MIX PHILOSOPHY

- **VO always sits on top**, but never fights the music — sidechain gentle ducking on score during VO lines, released fully during the two "silence" moments (00:23–00:25 and 01:28–01:30).
- **SFX should feel tactile and close-mic'd** in Act 1 (paper, wind, breath) to create intimacy, then gradually widen into more spacious, produced ambiences through Act 2 and 3 — sonic mirror of the visual scale expansion.
- **The custom tablet power-on tone (00:25) is the single most valuable audio asset produced for this campaign** — budget dedicated composer/sound-designer time to get it right, and plan for its reuse as the brand's permanent sonic identity across app, web, and future ads.
- **Final delivery:** Mix to broadcast/streaming loudness standards (-23 LUFS broadcast / -14 LUFS streaming as separate masters), plus a dedicated silent/captioned cutdown for social feeds where autoplay-muted viewing is the default (see `07-Production-Workflow.md` for deliverables list).
