# TRACKFARMOPS — CONTINUITY BIBLE
"The Land Remembers" — 90-Second Commercial

This document exists to protect the film's single most important storytelling device: **visual and physical consistency across a non-linear shoot schedule spanning multiple locations, weeks, and units.** Cross-reference constantly with `02-Character-Bible.md`, `05-Shot-Bible.md`, and `08-Asset-Tracker.md`.

## 1. THE FARMER — MASTER CONTINUITY RECORD

| Attribute | Value (locked) | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Wardrobe | Canvas jacket (W-01), boots (W-02) — identical, unwashed/unaltered | Every scene, Act 1 through Act 3 |
| Satchel state — Act 1 | Full, straining, strap groove worn into shoulder | A1-01 through A1-04 |
| Satchel state — post-spill | Empty, held loosely in hand or set aside | A1-04 onward (notebooks now on the ground/dissolved) |

| Hand condition | Cracked knuckles, dirt under nails — maintain with makeup department touch-ups each day, do not let hands look "cleaner" in later-shot-but-earlier-in-story scenes | All scenes |

**Critical scheduling risk:** Because Act 3 (golden hour, Day 6) is likely shot before or after Act 1 (dawn, Day 1) depending on weather windows, the makeup/wardrobe department must maintain IDENTICAL grooming and wardrobe wear regardless of actual shoot order. Take a full 360° reference photo set of The Farmer on Day 1 before any shooting begins, and check against it every subsequent day.

## 3. RECURRING SECONDARY CHARACTERS

Two ecosystem-cast members appear twice each (once in their dedicated Act 2 beat, once in the Act 2 cross-cut) — continuity must match exactly between these paired appearances.

| Character | First appearance | Second appearance | Match requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warehouse Officer | A2-05 (dedicated beat) | A2-11 (ecosystem cutaway) | Same actor, same wardrobe (hi-vis vest, gloves, headset), same tablet prop. Hairstyle/facial hair must not change between shoot days if these are shot separately. |
| Veterinarian | A2-09 (dedicated beat) | A2-11 (ecosystem cutaway) | Same actor, same coveralls/stethoscope, same tablet prop. If shot on different days, re-check against Day 1 reference photo. |

**Recommendation:** Schedule these two actors' A2-11 cutaway coverage on the SAME shoot day as their dedicated Act 2 beat (see Production Workflow Day 4 grouping) to minimize continuity risk. If this is not possible due to location logistics (A2-11 cutaways may be consolidated into fewer locations), take full reference photography at the end of their first shoot day.

## 4. THE FARMER'S PHYSICALITY ARC — SCENE-BY-SCENE CHECKLIST

Cross-reference Character Bible §1 physicality table. Because this arc must read as continuous even across a non-chronological shoot order, use this checklist to direct posture on every single take:

| Scene | Posture cue | Eyeline |
|---|---|---|
| A1-01, A1-02 | Weighted walk, shoulders forward | Down/ahead on path, not horizon |
| A1-03, A1-04 | Slight relief setting satchel down, but still heavy overall | Down at satchel/notebooks |
| A1-05 | Still, heavy | Down |
| A1-09 | Heaviest point — full stillness | Down at notebooks, held beat |
| A2-01 | First upward eyeline of the film — looking at tablet light | Up at screen (small, contained shift) |
| A2-02, A2-03 | Shoulders beginning to release, walking with slightly more purpose | Ahead, engaged with environment |
| A2-04 through A2-09 | Incrementally more upright each beat — track this like a dial, not discrete steps | Ahead, increasingly confident |
| A2-10, A2-11 (owner cutaway if used) | Fully upright, centered, composed | Direct at central display |
| A3-01 | Fully released — weightless, shoulders back | Ahead, open |
| A3-02 | Same, relaxed | Surveying the animated landscape |
| A3-03 | Upward, into sun flare | Up |

**Direction note for actor:** Think of this less as "acting confidence" and more as literally practicing the physical sensation of a weight being removed from one shoulder, incrementally, scene by scene. Physical rehearsal with an actual weighted satchel (removed gradually across rehearsal, not shoot) is recommended prior to Day 1.

## 5. LIGHT & COLOR CONTINUITY CROSS-CHECK

Since Act 2's five beats (Income/Expenses → Inventory → Assets → Livestock → Control Room) are likely shot across multiple days/locations (Production Workflow Days 3–5), color and light continuity must be tracked deliberately rather than assumed from the color grade alone:

| Beat | Target on-set warmth/saturation (relative, 1=Act 1 baseline, 10=full Act 3) | Practical light source to include on set |
|---|---|---|
| A2-02/03 (Income/Expenses) | 3/10 | Tablet screen glow, warm practical desk lamp |
| A2-04/05 (Inventory) | 4/10 | Warehouse tag glow, wall display glow |
| A2-06/07 (Assets) | 5/10 | Tractor tag glow, natural daylight slightly warmed |
| A2-08/09 (Livestock) | 6/10 | Tablet glow, natural daylight further warmed |
| A2-10/11 (Control Room) | 8/10 | Full central display glow + daylight bleed through glass walls |
| A3-01/02/03 (Ridge) | 10/10 | Full golden hour natural light, no artificial supplement needed |

DP and gaffer should treat this table as a lighting continuity script independent of the final grade — get as much of this warmth progression in-camera as possible rather than relying entirely on post color correction, per Visual Bible §3 (practical light sources should be real, not composited).

## 6. PROPS THAT MUST NEVER CROSS SCENES

To protect the notebook-to-light narrative logic (Continuity §2), the following props must be treated as strictly single-use per act:

- The six notebooks (P-02 through P-07): Act 1 only. Never appear again after A1-09.
- The cooperative's mismatched ledgers (P-10): A1-06 only. Visually distinct from the Farmer's own notebooks — do not let art department accidentally reuse a Farmer notebook prop as a cooperative ledger.
- The investor's folder (P-11): A1-07 only.
- The physical wall map (P-09): A1-08 only.
