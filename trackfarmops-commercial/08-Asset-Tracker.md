## 2. NOTEBOOK CONTINUITY MATRIX

All 6 notebooks must be visually distinct from their very first frame (per Character Bible §2, Script Production Notes) so viewers subconsciously register "six things" without counting. Lock these details before Day 1 and do not deviate.

| Notebook | Color | Distinguishing damage | First appears | Reforms as (Act 2 beat) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N1 (Financial — Income) | Dark brown leather-look | Rain-stained cover, warped pages | A1-04 | Income ledger (A2-03) |
| N2 (Financial — Expenses) | Faded red/maroon cloth | Torn page, missing corner | A1-04 | Expense ledger (A2-03) |
| N3 (Inventory) | Olive/khaki canvas | Grain dust ground into cover texture | A1-04 | Inventory tags (A2-05) |
| N4 (Assets) | Black, oil-stained | Grease fingerprints, bent spine | A1-04 | Asset tag (A2-07) |
| N5 (Livestock) | Tan, mud-spattered | Water-warped bottom edge | A1-04 | Livestock tag (A2-09) |
| N6 (Workforce/misc) | Grey, most weathered | Broken spine, held together with string/tape | A1-04 | Not directly dramatized on screen — implied within the "one truth" ecosystem beat (A2-11) |

**Rule:** By the control room scene (A2-10), none of the six notebooks should be visible anywhere in frame — their absence is the entire point (Script Production Notes). Confirm art department strikes all notebook props from that set.
