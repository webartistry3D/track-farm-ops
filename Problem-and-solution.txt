1. Poor Financial Visibility
Many farmers cannot answer these questions:
- How much did we earn this month?
- How much did we spend?
- Which farm unit is profitable?
- Where is money leaking?

2. Weak Inventory Control
They experience difficulty tracking:
- Feed
- Fertilizers
- Chemicals usage
- Seeds 
- Harvested produce
- Consumables
leading to losses, theft, and wastage.

3. Poor Asset Management 
They often struggle to monitor:
- Tractors
- Irrigation systems
- Generators
- Vehicles
- Processing equipment
including maintenance schedules and depreciation.

4. Workforce Accountability
Managers have limited visibility into:
- Worker attendance
- Assigned tasks
- Task completion
- Daily field activities

5. Fragmented Operations
Information is scattered across:
- WhatsApp chats
- Exercise books
- Excel sheets
- Individual managers
