# Scene 07 — Call to Action

## Objective

Implement **Scene 07**, the final scene of the TrackFarmOps cinematic landing experience. This scene concludes the story, reinforces the product's value, and drives visitors toward a clear conversion action.

## Story

The enterprise network gently fades into a beautiful aerial view of a thriving Nigerian farm at sunrise. The environment feels calm, organized, and optimistic. The TrackFarmOps logo, value proposition, and primary call-to-action take center stage, leaving visitors with a lasting impression of confidence and transformation.

The story ends where it began—but this time, the farm is powered by TrackFarmOps.

## Requirements

Build only Scene 07.

This is the final scene of the landing experience.
