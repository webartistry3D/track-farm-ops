# Illustration Style
## TrackFarmOps Landing Experience

> Version: 1.0
> Status: Locked
> Purpose: Define the illustration language and visual identity for all generated assets.
> Owner: WebArtistry Creations

---

# Philosophy

Illustrations are not decorative.

They are storytelling devices.

Every illustration exists to explain an idea, reinforce an emotion or support a narrative transition.

The illustration style should communicate:

Authenticity

Clarity

Modernity

Trust

Agricultural expertise

Technological confidence

Every illustration should feel like it belongs to one cinematic universe.

---

# Creative Vision

The illustration style should sit between:

Editorial Illustration

×

Semi-Realistic Digital Painting

×

Premium SaaS Product Illustration

The result should feel timeless.

Avoid trends that will look dated within a few years.

---

# Artistic Identity

Think:

Apple simplicity

Stripe clarity

Notion restraint

National Geographic authenticity

Modern African agriculture

Not:

Corporate stock illustration

Cartoons

Comic books

Gaming concept art

Anime

Cyberpunk

3D toy aesthetics

---

# Rendering Style

Semi-realistic

Soft painterly finish

Clean edges

Natural proportions

Subtle texture

Moderate detail

Readable silhouettes

No exaggerated realism.

No flat cartoon vectors.

The goal is believable elegance.

---

# Characters

Characters represent real agricultural professionals.

They should feel competent.

Focused.

Confident.

Respectful.

Avoid exaggerated expressions.

Avoid unrealistic fashion.

Avoid theatrical poses.

---

# Character Diversity

Represent the reality of Nigerian agriculture.

Include:

Commercial farm managers

Female accountants

Agronomists

Warehouse supervisors

Field workers

Equipment operators

Produce inspectors

Cooperative leaders

The landing page should celebrate African agriculture without stereotyping it.

---

# Facial Expressions

Preferred

Focused

Thoughtful

Satisfied

Determined

Hopeful

Avoid

Overly excited

Comedic

Confused

Angry

Exaggerated smiles

Subtle confidence communicates professionalism.

---

# Clothing

Realistic work attire.

Examples:

Reflective safety vest

Field boots

Farm cap

Polo shirt

Branded workwear

Casual office attire

Avoid:

Business suits in rice fields

Laboratory coats

Fashion photography styling

Unrealistic uniforms

---

# Environments

Illustrations should depict:

Rice fields

Storage warehouses

Farm offices

Processing facilities

Equipment yards

Harvest staging areas

Loading zones

Everything should feel like a real Nigerian commercial farming operation.

---

# Agriculture Accuracy

Depict:

Real irrigation layouts

Rice paddies

Storage bags

Farm roads

Local pickup trucks

Tractors appropriate for regional use

Fertilizer sacks

Harvest crates

Avoid generic Western farming imagery.

Research before illustrating.

---

# Dashboard Integration

The dashboard should naturally coexist with illustrations.

Never float randomly.

Never appear holographic.

It should feel like a tool being used by real people.

The product remains grounded.

---

# Object Design

Every object should have:

Simple geometry

Readable silhouette

Consistent scale

Consistent perspective

Natural materials

Objects should be recognizable instantly.

---

# Color Application

Illustrations follow the Color System.

Muted earth tones.

Natural greens.

Soft whites.

Warm sunlight.

Restrained accent colors.

Avoid saturated palettes.

---

# Lighting

Use the Lighting specification.

Every illustration must maintain:

Upper-left sunlight

Soft shadows

Warm daylight

Natural exposure

Consistent atmospheric perspective

---

# Texture

Low-frequency texture only.

Clean surfaces.

Subtle grain.

Minimal painterly brush texture.

Avoid:

Heavy texture overlays

Noise

Grunge

Rough concept-art finishes

---

# Perspective

Primary perspective:

Eye level

Secondary:

Slight aerial

Occasional:

Drone establishing shots

Avoid:

Extreme fisheye

Dutch angles

Wide-angle distortion

The illustrations should feel observational.

---

# Scale

Human scale matters.

Buildings should feel believable.

Machinery should have realistic proportions.

The farm should communicate openness.

Never compress space unnaturally.

---

# Background Treatment

Backgrounds support.

They never compete.

Reduce detail as depth increases.

Foreground receives the highest clarity.

Atmospheric perspective should separate layers naturally.

---

# Motion Readiness

Every illustration should be designed with animation in mind.

Separate visual layers where possible:

Foreground

Characters

Documents

Equipment

Vegetation

Sky

This enables parallax and GSAP motion without redrawing assets.

---

# AI Generation Guidelines

Every prompt should include:

Semi-realistic editorial illustration

Modern African commercial agriculture

Natural daylight

Soft shadows

Warm color grading

Minimal visual clutter

Premium SaaS aesthetic

Consistent perspective

No text

No watermarks

No logos

No dramatic effects

Every prompt should reference this document.

# Consistency Rules

Every generated asset must match:

Lighting

Perspective

Rendering quality

Character proportions

Brush style

Edge treatment

Color grading

Material response

Visual hierarchy

Assets from different AI models should be indistinguishable after post-processing.

---

# Asset Categories

Illustration standards apply equally to:

Characters

Farm machinery

Buildings

Paper documents

Warehouse interiors

Fields

Maps

Icons

Environmental assets

Dashboard overlays

No category receives a different artistic treatment.

---

# Quality Checklist

Before approving any illustration, verify:

✓ Feels authentically Nigerian

✓ Supports the story

✓ Matches the lighting guide

✓ Matches the color system

✓ Uses the correct perspective

✓ Maintains the established rendering style

✓ Can be animated cleanly

✓ Does not resemble stock artwork

---

# Final Principle

A visitor should be able to see any single illustration—without the TrackFarmOps logo—and immediately recognize that it belongs to the same visual universe.

The illustrations should feel handcrafted, authentic and purposeful.

They should never merely decorate the page.

They should help tell the story of transforming African farm management from chaos into clarity.
