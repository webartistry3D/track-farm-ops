# Lighting
## TrackFarmOps Landing Experience

> Version: 1.0
> Status: Locked
> Purpose: Define the lighting language of the TrackFarmOps cinematic experience.
> Owner: WebArtistry Creations

---

# Philosophy

Lighting is emotion.

Before visitors consciously recognize colors, typography or animation, they subconsciously respond to light.

Lighting should guide emotion, direct attention and reinforce the narrative.

The lighting should always feel natural.

Never theatrical.

Never artificial.

---

# Lighting Goals

Every scene should communicate:

Authenticity

Hope

Professionalism

Warmth

Trust

Optimism

TrackFarmOps solves problems.

The lighting should reflect that optimism.

---

# Master Lighting Direction

Primary Light Source

Natural Sunlight

Direction

Upper Left (preferred)

Elevation

35°–45°

Temperature

Warm Morning Light

Consistency

The primary light direction must remain constant throughout the experience.

Shadows should always behave consistently.

---

# Lighting Progression

Scene 1

Golden sunrise.

Soft long shadows.

Gentle highlights.

The farm feels peaceful.

---

Scene 2

Sun remains.

Contrast increases.

Shadows become slightly stronger.

Documents cast layered shadows.

Visual tension increases without changing the time of day.

---

Scene 3

Light returns to balance.

Dashboard emits subtle internal glow.

The environment feels cleaner.

Shadows soften.

The visitor experiences relief.

---

Scene 4

Bright neutral daylight.

Minimal shadow complexity.

Everything feels organized.

---

Scene 5

Consistent daylight.

Charts and dashboards receive subtle emphasis through brightness.

No dramatic lighting.

The interface becomes the brightest object.

---

Scene 6

Open sky.

Expanded horizon.

Large soft light.

The world feels bigger.

Hope replaces tension.

---

Scene 7

Minimal background.

Balanced white environment.

Soft natural lighting.

Nothing distracts from the CTA.

---

# Natural Lighting Rules

Prefer:

Morning light

Overcast softness

Golden agricultural atmosphere

Ambient sky illumination

Avoid:

Night scenes

Harsh midday sun

Storm lighting

Extreme contrast

Blue-tinted environments

Artificial neon lighting

---

# Dashboard Lighting

The UI should never glow like a sci-fi interface.

Instead:

Soft elevation

Natural shadow

Subtle highlights

Light surfaces

The dashboard should appear tangible.

---

# Shadow System

Shadow Opacity

8–16%

Blur

Large

Edges

Soft

Direction

Matches primary light source

Avoid:

Black shadows

Sharp edges

Floating effects

Heavy ambient occlusion

---

# Atmospheric Perspective

Distance should be created through:

Reduced contrast

Softer detail

Lower saturation

Gentle haze

Never through artificial blur alone.

---

# Reflections

Allowed only when physically justified.

Examples:

Glass on tablets

Phone screens

Water

Metal machinery

Avoid decorative reflections.

---

# Visual Focus

The brightest area should always be:

The subject

OR

The TrackFarmOps interface

Never the background.

Light directs attention.

---

# Character Lighting

Characters should feel integrated into the environment.

No studio portrait lighting.

No dramatic rim lighting.

No fashion photography.

Use soft daylight with natural skin tones representative of Nigerian farmers and professionals.

---

# Environmental Lighting

Rice fields

Warm highlights

Warehouse

Soft indirect light

Office

Bright neutral daylight

Dashboard

Natural interface illumination

Maps

Balanced ambient light

Every environment should feel physically believable.

---

# AI Asset Consistency

Every generated image must maintain:

Identical light direction

Consistent shadow softness

Matching color temperature

Unified exposure

Similar contrast

This ensures assets from different tools appear as one cohesive production.

---

# Lighting Anti-Patterns

Never use:

❌ Neon glows

❌ Heavy bloom

❌ Lens flares

❌ Artificial HDR extremes

❌ Colored shadows

❌ Sci-fi holographic lighting

❌ Flash photography aesthetics

---

# Final Principle

Lighting should never announce itself.

It should quietly reinforce one feeling:

"This is a real farm entering a brighter, more organized future."
