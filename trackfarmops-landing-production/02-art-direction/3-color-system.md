> Version: 1.0
> Status: Locked
> Purpose: Brand Color System & Emotional Color Direction

---

# Philosophy

Color communicates emotion before words.

Every color used throughout the landing experience should reinforce the emotional journey from chaos to clarity.

Avoid decorative color.

Use color intentionally.

# Shadows

Use soft shadows.

Never harsh black shadows.

Prefer:

8–16% opacity

Large blur radius

Natural elevation

---

# Gradients

Gradients are allowed only when they represent:

Sunlight

Natural lighting

Subtle depth

Never use gradients as decoration.

---

# Accessibility

All color combinations must meet WCAG AA standards.

Interactive states must never rely on color alone.

---

# Color Anti-Patterns

Never use:

❌ Neon greens

❌ Oversaturated colors

❌ Heavy gradients

❌ Random accent colors

❌ Excessive glass effects

❌ Pure black backgrounds

❌ Corporate blue dominance

The landing page should always feel grounded in agriculture.

---

# Final Principle

The visitor should never consciously think about the colors.

They should simply feel the transition:

From uncertainty...

To clarity...

From paperwork...

To productivity...

Color exists to guide that emotional transformation.
