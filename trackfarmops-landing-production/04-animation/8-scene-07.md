# Engineering Notes

Avoid common SaaS landing page patterns such as flashing buttons, countdowns or aggressive conversion tactics.

This scene should earn the user's action through everything that came before it.

The animation should conclude naturally, leaving the visitor with a sense of confidence and possibility.
