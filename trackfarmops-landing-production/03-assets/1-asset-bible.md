# Purpose

The Asset Bible is the definitive inventory of every visual asset used throughout the production.

If an asset appears anywhere in the experience, it must exist here.

This document ensures:

• Nothing is forgotten

• Nothing is duplicated unnecessarily

• Assets remain consistent

• Production can be planned accurately

• AI generation can be executed systematically

# Final Principle

The Asset Bible is the single source of truth for every visual element in the TrackFarmOps landing experience.

Nothing enters production unless it exists here.

Nothing is removed from production without updating this document.
