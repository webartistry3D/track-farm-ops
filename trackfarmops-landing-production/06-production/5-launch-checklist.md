# Purpose

This document defines the final verification process before the TrackFarmOps landing experience is released to production.

No item in this checklist may be skipped.

Launch readiness requires approval across engineering, design, performance, accessibility and business.

---

# Launch Philosophy

Build carefully.

Test relentlessly.

Launch confidently.

Monitor continuously.

A successful launch is not the end of production.

It is the beginning of the product's public life.

# Launch Success Criteria

The launch is considered successful when:

✓ The landing experience is publicly accessible.

✓ Every cinematic scene performs as designed.

✓ Performance budgets remain within target.

✓ Accessibility standards are maintained.

✓ Core business journeys complete successfully.

✓ Monitoring confirms a stable production environment.

✓ The experience communicates TrackFarmOps' value from the first scroll to the final call-to-action.
